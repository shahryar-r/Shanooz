# Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 6, 2026

## Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Shanooz ("the Application", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use our application.

Your privacy is important to us. Shanooz is designed to provide news content while respecting your privacy.

## Information We Collect

Shanooz does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information from users.

We do not collect:

* Names
* Email addresses
* Phone numbers
* Location information
* Contacts
* User accounts or login information
* Device identifiers
* Advertising identifiers
* Usage analytics
* Tracking information

We do not create user profiles or track users' activities within the application.

## Data Stored on Your Device

Shanooz may store news articles, application settings, and user preferences locally on your device.

This locally stored information is used only to provide app functionality, such as:

* Displaying downloaded news articles
* Remembering application settings
* Improving your reading experience

This data remains on your device and is not sent to us.

## News Content and Network Requests

Shanooz connects to external news feeds and services to retrieve news articles and related content.

These requests are used only to provide news content within the application. Shanooz does not use these requests to identify, track, or collect information about users.

Any information that may be automatically handled by third-party servers during normal internet communication is subject to the privacy policies of those services.

## Third-Party Services

Shanooz does not use third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or user profiling services.

## Children's Privacy

Shanooz does not knowingly collect any personal information from children or any users.

Because we do not collect personal information, there is no user data stored or processed by us.

## Data Security

Since Shanooz does not collect or store personal information, we do not maintain user databases containing personal data.

We recommend keeping your device secure by using appropriate device protection measures.

## Your Privacy Rights

Because Shanooz does not collect personal information, there is no personal data held by us to access, modify, or delete.

If you have questions about privacy or this policy, you can contact us using the information below.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
